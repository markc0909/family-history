Search coverage, not proof of absence

Yad Vashem surname/place searches yielded six Lederer victim results and the Chaya Dwora Milstein grouping. A Nutke/Milstein synonym search returned twenty-two victim results; none was safely joined to the family. A further father-Leib/mother-Chaya filter narrowed sixty-two entries to eight without a secure match. The wider sixty-two were not exhaustively reviewed. Brakha Berland’s submitter-name search returned twenty-nine records, with no additional explicit Hela/Doba link established. These are limits of the research, not evidence of survival.Mark Cohen’s family recollections
