Whose story, and whose words?

Mark Cohen assembled the family documents, recordings and recollections used in this collection. The earlier fifteen-page Riva Gilinsky PDF was written by his son, Ethan Cohen. Its first-person statements are Ethan’s: Riva is his great-grandmother, and phrases such as “my father” refer to his father, Mark. A source and an identification are different

A civil record may securely say that Morris Millstein died on a particular date. Whether that Morris is our Moshe is a separate question. This archive marks that identification as strong while keeping the missing family-name link visible. How citations and saved files work

Claim-level source links lead to the catalogue, which gives the title, locator and access notes. The catalogue links to the source website and to a saved copy where available. FamilySearch may require sign-in; a saved browser screenshot is labelled accordingly. Restricted estate records have catalogue links rather than invented downloads.

PDF extracts preserve the relevant pages; full public PDFs are also bundled where accessible. Display images are resized for the web, while the saved document files retain the supplied scans or downloaded bytes. The asset register records origin, file size and SHA-256 checksums. A downloaded copy does not imply unrestricted reuse rights.

Recordings and translations

The working analysis used local automatic speech transcription. It is not a certified verbatim transcript or a verified speaker identification. Name readings, quiet speech, jokes and overlaps require human review. Passage guides point to the recording rather than presenting uncertain text as a direct quotation. Apparent duplicate tapes are labelled and are not counted as independent corroboration.

The Yahad clip was reviewed through embedded English subtitles, not independently translated. The video remains linked to its source; its complete transcript is not reproduced. Handwritten Polish, Hebrew and Yiddish names are kept provisional where reading is uncertain.

What this edition contains

This edition synthesises the family material and online research checked on 18–19 November 2026. It includes corrections to the older written history, an open-questions register and source-access limits. Original outbound estate-request correspondence, email identifiers, unreviewed raw conversation transcripts and speculative allegations are not part of the published pages. Relevant historical findings from those conversations are incorporated with appropriate attribution.

Living descendants are not expanded into new detailed profiles in this edition. Ethan’s original PDF is retained as the supplied historical compilation, including its original tree; its older “Living” labels and uncertain dates are explained in the corrections register.

Contributing the next piece

A good addition is specific: an original document, a full photograph reverse, a name with its spelling, a recording passage, or a correction with a source. Keep the original file, record its owner or archive, and distinguish what it says from what you infer. Future branches can share existing people and sources rather than duplicating them.
