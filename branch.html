Milstein & Lederer / The Opalin collection

From Opalin,
across generations.

A family story carried from a small town in Volhynia to southern Africa and Israel, preserved in passports, photographs and recorded memories.

Explore the treeFollow the timelineHear the familySee the open questions

The central household is Moshe Milstein and Doba Lederer, and their children Aron (Arke), Riva (Masie), Ben (Saul) and Philip (Pinchas). Moshe and Arke went ahead; Doba’s passport and the 1934 manifest record her travelling with the three younger children.uncle ben interview - 1Doba Milsztejn’s Polish passportGloucester Castle passenger manifest, 1934Uncle Ben – 1, 04:24–05:35

The family’s recollections reach beyond that household: Minnie tells of the Milsteins and Appelbaums; Hela recalls her Lederer childhood and Warsaw uncle; Israel Alter’s memoir helps connect the family across the Bug and onward to Israel.
