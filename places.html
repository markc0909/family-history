Opalin / Vyshnivka

Family home; today Volyn, Ukraine. The Yahad profile identifies the former town. Do not merge with Ruda Opalin in Poland.Doba Milsztejn’s Polish passportVyshnivka, formerly Opalin — killing-site profile

Warsaw

Mendel’s bakery appears at Ogrodowa 24 in 1928. Doba’s settlement and transit visas were issued here.Mendel Lederer’s Warsaw bakery, 1928South African settlement visaGerman transit visa

Zbąszyń

The Polish exit stamp gives 6 September 1934. It is a border-control locator, not a full rail itinerary.Polish border-control stamp

London

The manifest records embarkation here on 13 September 1934. The tapes recall a shelter and hospitality but do not securely identify the address.Gloucester Castle passenger manifest, 1934Talking of Opalin - 1

Cape Town & Germiston

Cape Town was the family’s contracted landing port. Ben recalls Moshe meeting the train at Germiston on the way to Johannesburg.Gloucester Castle passenger manifest, 1934uncle ben interview - 2

Troyeville / Mayfair / Greenside / Emmarentia

The candidate Morris death and Mina marriage share 31 Bezuidenhout Street, Troyeville. Later neighbourhood moves come from testimony; Doba’s estate notice gives 36 Umgeni Road, Emmarentia.Morris Millstein death certificate, 1935Mina Milstein and Elias Spitalnik, 1937uncle ben interview - 2Doba Slot estate notice, 2783/77

Bulawayo

Harry moved in November 1937 in Hela’s account; Hela and Phyllis followed in June 1938. Scott Street and Glen Eagles belong to their story.Hela Lederer Tape 1 - 2Hela Lederer Tape 1 - 1

Dorohusk / Maciejow

Chaya Dwora’s prewar and wartime places in her testimony. Israel Alter’s memoir is in the Dorohusk memorial-book section.Chaya Dwora Alter, born MilshteinYisrael Alter’s Dorohusk memoir

Tel Aviv / Palestine

Israel describes arrival in Palestine in 1936. The Film Archive places Naftali’s birth in Tel Aviv in 1947; Mark visited Israel there in 1986.Yisrael Alter’s Dorohusk memoirNaftali Alter — Israel Film ArchiveMark Cohen’s family recollections

Denver / Buenos Aires

Separate collateral leads: Hela’s maternal aunt Emma in Denver; an unnamed Lederer son associated with Buenos Aires. Neither is a verified household here.Hela Lederer Tape 1 - 2Talking of Opalin - 1Minnie Spitalnik - 2

Wulka / Volka

The Appelbaum village is only an uncertain sound in the recording. No coordinates or modern identity are assigned.Minnie Spitalnik - 2
